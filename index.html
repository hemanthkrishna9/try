About ChitraYoga

Hi, I’m Chitrika

Founder & Yoga Instructor

A horticulture graduate who discovered yoga as a personal practice and gradually turned her passion into a profession. With years of personal practice, she developed an interest in making yoga accessible, understandable and sustainable for everyday life.

Through ChitraYoga, she aims to help people build a consistent yoga practice that supports strength, flexibility, stamina, body awareness, breathing and overall well-being.

Chitrika

My Teaching Philosophy

“Sthiram Sukham Asanam”

Finding steadiness and ease in a posture.

Yoga is not about achieving one perfect pose or looking flexible; it is about understanding your body, practising with awareness, and developing a lifelong relationship with movement and breath.

FAQs

I’m not flexible. Can I join?

Yes. Yoga builds flexibility over time. Every class offers options for different levels.

Do I need a mat?

A mat is recommended.

I’m a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Yes. Beginners are welcome in all group batches, and the pace is guided step by step.

Can I join from outside India?

Yes. Online classes work for students in any time zone, and 1:1 sessions can be scheduled around you.

Can I join mid-month?

Yes, absolutely — every class is different.

What happens in a class?

Centering, pranayama, warm-up, asana practice, and a cool-down with savasana.

Is there a trial class?

Yes, a trial class is available at a charge. Message on Instagram for details.

Can I join if I have back pain or another health concern?

Please share relevant concerns before joining. The suitability of yoga and the appropriate modifications depend on your individual circumstances. Yoga classes do not replace medical assessment or treatment.
